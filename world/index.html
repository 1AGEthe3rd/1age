1AGE WORLD

A platform you can explore.

1AGE World is the immersive extension of 1AGE: connected destinations for identity, social connection, games, commerce, learning and creation.

Built from a working foundation.

1AGEWorld is the native Godot prototype behind the visual direction. It already supports walking, flying and virtual touch controls. The public browser experience is still in development.

The interactive globe on the homepage is a lightweight project navigator. It does not load a full 3D world.
