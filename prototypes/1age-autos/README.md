# Spatial Design 015 — 1AGE Autos
Founder-approved global automotive platform. Prestige cosmic showroom with five stations: global discovery, dealerships, test-drive lab, parts/accessories, peer-to-peer commerce. Future interactive Earth and geographic discovery for real vehicles, dealerships, importers and parts. No live listings or verified inventory yet.

Space owns the location/virtual showroom record; Autos indexes vehicle listings and driving profiles; Shop provides the shared merchant, parts, fitment, stock and order catalog. Dealerships may participate without renting Space. A single dealership can have multiple authorized locations and one portable inventory. Proposed Javan Imports research pilot in HQ1 Portland, not an endorsement, verified partnership, inventory claim or authorized digital twin. Preserve HQ1 assets and controls. No real business data imported.

Driving lab: three fictional profiles with mass, power, drivetrain, wheelbase, grip, steering and braking parameters. Simple forward-only bicycle-style kinematic simulation, bounded test disk, primitive vehicles and cabin details, right/left steering wheel placement, chase/driver/hood views. Shared left stick steers horizontally and accelerates/brakes vertically; keyboard WASD/arrows, Space brakes, C cycles camera. No gas/brake labels. Controller mapping inherits shared adapter. Not manufacturer calibrated; no realistic tire/suspension/gearbox, reverse, crash model or licensed car meshes yet. Right-stick look and full driving gamepad layout remain future work. Visual quality is a prototype, not HQ1 parity.

Future: verified real vehicle specs and licensed models, geographic inventory and vehicle/parts fitment, merchant permissions, seller verification, inspections, rental/purchase inquiry, lawful transfer and title checks. Blockchain may support receipts and provenance, but does not replace government title/registration, payment authorization or identity. Exchange and regulated payment partners for supported settlement; no custody, escrow, live crypto transactions, listings or payments enabled.
