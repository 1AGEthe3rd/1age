# Spatial Design 008 — 1AGE Work
Independent walkable employment-center prototype staged in the central 1age repository because no standalone Work repository was accessible and repository creation is unavailable through this connector. New files only under prototypes/1age-work. Do not merge into or alter the homepage; migrate to the standalone Work repository with required shared assets after founder approval. No live domain change.

## Product direction
Remote, virtual and blockchain-industry employment across conventional job categories. Simple employer listings, opt-in application through 1AGE Page, freelancer discovery and employment/income records. Work is professional employment and contracts; Quest is community missions. Current prototype provides synthetic role filtering, local posting preview, a Page link and an educational records station. It does not accept real listings, applications, sensitive data, payments or credentials.

## Future employment evidence
Use verified employer identities and authorized payroll or contractor records; do not infer employment from a wallet transfer. Keep distinct employee pay statements, employment confirmation, and contractor invoice/income records. Include relevant jurisdiction, pay period, currency, gross/net amounts, deductions, source, issuer, reconciliation status and correction history. For lawful crypto settlement record the chain, transaction reference, asset and quantity, confirmation, and documented local-currency fair market valuation with timestamp/source. Blockchain evidence is supplemental to authoritative records.
Private access by default; no wages, legal names, housing applications or other personal data on public chains. Future worker-authorized limited sharing, issuer signature verification, revocation/expiry, access audit and correction/dispute workflow. Explore W3C verifiable credentials (https://www.w3.org/TR/vc-data-model/) rather than inventing a credential format. Landlords/lenders decide acceptance; no guarantee of housing eligibility. No fake pay stubs, self-certified employment, or public wallet history as employment proof.
US tax reference: https://www.irs.gov/filing/digital-assets ; jurisdiction-specific wage/payment, payroll, withholding, worker classification, privacy, screening/consumer reporting, and financial-service requirements require review before launch. Use licensed/qualified providers where necessary. No live crypto payouts or funds custody.
