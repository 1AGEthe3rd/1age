# Spatial Design 019 — 1AGE Services
Founder-approved global peer-to-peer service platform for individuals and traditional or blockchain businesses. Independent walkable futuristic cosmic center with global discovery, local services, remote services, booking center and provider studio. Existing shared walking controls, local Three.js, Earth texture and logo. Searchable illustrative service categories, local request/appointment concept and provider listing composer. No real providers, appointment slots, prices, submissions or payments.

Future shared Page identity and business profile, provider-owned catalog, geographic service areas (manual city selection; location optional), remote delivery, quotes, appointment calendars, communication and authorized payments. Space premises are optional; World can connect participating physical and virtual venues to the same service record. Work owns employment and professional work opportunities; Design owns creative project discovery; Shop owns products. Service provider licensing/qualification, consent, privacy, safety, cancellation/refunds, moderation, review integrity and applicable regulated services require verification before public launch. Free ordinary discovery/listing is a proposed starting model; no pricing or fees enabled.

Temporary isolated prototype in central repository because no accessible dedicated Services repository exists. Do not merge into the homepage. Migrate to a standalone repository with shared dependencies and review actual domain configuration before release. No root, HQ, World, DNS or production edits. Browser rendering, iPhone and controller tests pending. No public posting, booking or commerce.
