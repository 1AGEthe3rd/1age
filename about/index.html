ABOUT 1AGE

An easier way into Solana.

Our mission is to organize the Solana ecosystem, making it easier to discover, accessible to everyone, and useful for all.

Discovery comes first.

1AGE helps people find projects, communities, games and tools. It connects people to specialized services rather than recreating every service itself.

The current public directory is an early, small index. It will grow through research and review.

Web and World, together.

The platform is being developed around functional websites and an optional immersive layer called 1AGE World. Using the web platform should not require an avatar.

The visual foundation comes from 1AGE.Desktop and the working native 1AGEWorld prototype. A browser-based immersive room is not available on this site yet.
